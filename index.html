01 / The idea

Give intent a path
into the physical world.

A language model can decide what matters in a scene. ACDC gives that decision an execution harness: posed exploration frames, rendered feedback, and motion planning grounded in geometry.

Natural-language request+Posed exploration video+Mapped 3D scene

Defined workflow

The sequence is prescribed.

The model works through three fixed stages.

  1. 01
    Select & order shotsTurn intent into descriptions; retrieve posed frames.
  2. 02
    Refine each viewpointCompare rendered alternatives with preference-based Bayesian optimization.
  3. 03
    Plan the flightA*, corridors, and TOGT connect the views; interpolate camera yaw.

Agent-directed workflow

The agent chooses the next step.

After inspecting the result, the agent can revise the route, camera direction, timing, and tools.

Measurement method & experimental limits +

An episode is a contiguous sequence of sampled speeds strictly above 1 m/s. Speed is the norm of the finite-difference world-position derivative at the saved timestamps, without smoothing, gap merging, or a minimum episode length. These sampled diagnostics are not continuous-time certification.

The defined runs differ in fidelity: Opus reports 100 refinement comparisons per shot, Astra 20, Sol 3, and Sonnet 20–21. Some runs replace retrieval or reduce render quality. Agent-directed implementations also differ in planner, renderer, and clearance definition. This is a comparison of the supplied final runs, not a controlled, equal-budget ablation or a universal model ranking.

The videos are web-optimized encodes of the supplied finals, preserving resolution, frame rate, framing, and timing. Plots use the original CSVs, with extrema-preserving downsampling for display. Poster aggregates whose underlying evaluation files are absent are not used here.

04 / Visual preference refinement

Refine a view
through feedback.

Seven perturbed initializations approach the reference view in image-embedding space over 100 comparisons. The judge does not see the reference image.

This separate component experiment measures local recovery in embedding space, rather than human-rated cinematic quality.
