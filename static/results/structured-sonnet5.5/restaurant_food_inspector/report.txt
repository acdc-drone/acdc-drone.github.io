## How each stage went
- **Stage 1.** Environment description and shot descriptions written by me from 63/64 sampled frames (code's prompts). Seed retrieval: SigLIP2 text embeddings + saved image embeddings, top-8 per shot over every 5th frame. I then picked one frame per shot from contact sheets. 14/14 shots kept.
- **Stage 2.** ACDC's GP preference optimizer (imported unchanged), M=64 candidates, ρ_trans=1 m, ρ_rot=30°, one comparison per shot per round, judged by me from side-by-side renders. **280 comparisons in total, 59 challengers accepted, 117 ties** (20 per shot, not B=100). Every comparison, its decision and reason: `refine/state.json` (history), `refine/decisions/rNNN.txt`, sheets `refine/sheets/`. Refined poses: `refine/all_best_results.json`.
- **Stage 3.** ACDC code unchanged: A* + safe-corridor gates (`run_trajectory_generation.py`), TOGT (`run_togt_scenes.py`, including its own clearance re-optimization retries), RotationSpline camera orientation. Outputs in `stage3/`.

## Deviations from the design (and why)
1. **Refinement budget ~20 comparisons/shot instead of B=100.** I judge each pair by eye; 100 × ~14 shots × 6 runs was not possible. Refinement was also stopped when I ran out of time (a partly judged round was discarded).
2. **Optimizer perturbs the full 3-D rotation (code behaviour)**, whereas the paper says position+yaw with roll/pitch fixed to 0. I kept the code. Consequently roll/pitch in the final are not 0 (see checks); the saved ACDC output has the same property.
3. Pair judgments were made on a sheet showing incumbent (image 1) and challenger (image 2) side by side at 480×270 each, several shots per sheet; ties ("3") were allowed as in the code. Refinement renders used ACDC's own Blender script (8 spp CPU) on the patched mesh.
4. Environment description / shots were written once per scene and shared by the scene's two prompts. The last exploration frame was not viewed.
5. Text embedding: only the SigLIP2 text tower of the RADIO adaptor was run (fp16 weights, fp32 compute) to fit the memory cap; image embeddings and occupancy grids were the saved precomputed ones (grids built from the original meshes).
6. Final video: patched mesh + patched renderer (as instructed), via `tools/make_video_mine.py` (frames piped to ffmpeg in chunks to save disk); same resolution/fps/FOV as saved video.
7. A first retrieval attempt had a bug in my text-embedding wrapper (garbage embeddings); discarded and redone (`stage1/invalid_attempt1_meta_bug/`).
