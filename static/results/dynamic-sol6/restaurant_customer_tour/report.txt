# restaurant_customer_tour final report

**Prompt interpretation.** A customer-facing tour of tables, bar, and welcoming dining areas.

**Comparison with baseline.** The final follows the baseline's visit order where the clearance rule permits. Duration is 69.3 s versus 51.4 s. The final's measured top speed is 0.798 m/s versus 1.077 m/s; minimum 0.01 s sampled distance to occupied voxel centers is 0.323 m versus 0.221 m. Camera roll is 0° (baseline peak 7.3°); pitch stays within ±17.8° (baseline peak 18.0°). Peak camera yaw rate is 2.16 versus 2.61 rad/s. The final uses the patched mesh and renderer background at the baseline 960×540, 24 fps, 80° FOV. Its 512 px texture copy fits the resource cap but loses some fine detail compared with the original 2048 px textures.

**Checks.** The bundle's `validate_trajectory` passed with 17 explicit safe-route shot gates. Independent 0.01 s sampling found 0.323 m minimum voxel-center clearance; a speed-based bound between samples is 0.320 m. Maximum body tilt was 6.9°, the upper bound on body XY rate was 0.68 rad/s, peak camera yaw rate was 2.16 rad/s, and the estimated motor-thrust envelope was 1.78–2.42 N. The 1665-frame video was fully decoded and reset to an exact 24/1 average frame rate.
