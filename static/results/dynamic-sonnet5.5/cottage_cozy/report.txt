# cottage_cozy

## How the prompt was interpreted
'Complete tour of this house, emphasizing a cozy and comfortable vibe.' Interpreted as: visit every main space (living room, dining, bedroom + ensuite, laundry/second washroom, kitchen, breakfast nook) at low speed (<=0.55 m/s), lingering on soft furnishings (armchair, fireplace and pouf, sofa, bed, nook table), ending in the breakfast nook.

## Final trajectory (measured)
- Duration 115.8 s, 2782 frames (24 fps, 960x540, 80 deg FOV), path length 36.8 m.
- Bundle `validate_trajectory`: passed = True (0 occupied voxels, 0 outside grid; gate error ~0).
- Max speed 0.55 m/s (limit 1.0), max accel 0.32 m/s2, max tilt 1.9 deg, thrust/mass 9.8-9.9 m/s2 (daju limits: tilt 30 deg).
- Camera: roll 2.5e-14 deg (exactly 0), max |pitch| 16.1 deg, max yaw rate 25 deg/s (limit 3.0 rad/s).
- Clearance: min surface distance 0.34 m; min distance to occupied voxel centre 0.25 m; to voxel box 0.13 m.
  **The 0.3 m voxel clearance requirement is NOT fully met:** 2.4% of samples are below 0.3 m voxel-centre distance, only in passages that are physically ~0.6-1.0 m wide in the scan.

## Rendering
scene_patch meshes and renderer script (copied with one speed-up: Cycles on CPU, 12 samples/pixel instead of 256, because GPU rendering does not fit the 1.5 GB VRAM limit; 12 spp measured ~49 dB PSNR vs 256 spp on test frames), same camera/FOV/resolution/fps, mild unsharp mask as in the patched pipeline, libx264 CRF 18.

## Remaining flaws
The laundry/second-washroom doorway is only ~0.6 m wide in the scan, so it is viewed from the corridor, not entered. Corridor to the bedroom (~0.8 m wide) is below the 0.3 m voxel-centre clearance. TV above the fireplace is a black rectangle in the scan. Some windows/shutters show scan artefacts.
Visual review was done on contact sheets (frames every 1.5-5 s), not frame-by-frame.
