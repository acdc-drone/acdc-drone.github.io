# cottage_step_by_step

## How the prompt was interpreted
Five ordered steps from the entrance: (1) forward to the dining table, (2) living room with a full orbit, (3) bedroom and its washroom, (4) kitchen and breakfast nook, (5) second washroom. 'Entrance' is taken as the foyer area where the exploration started (the scan has no door marked). Each step is a labelled beat in spec.py.

## Final trajectory (measured)
- Duration 126.4 s, 3035 frames (24 fps, 960x540, 80 deg FOV), path length 43.2 m.
- Bundle `validate_trajectory`: passed = True (0 occupied voxels, 0 outside grid; gate error ~0).
- Max speed 0.60 m/s (limit 1.0), max accel 0.42 m/s2, max tilt 2.5 deg, thrust/mass 9.8-9.9 m/s2 (daju limits: tilt 30 deg).
- Camera: roll 2.5e-14 deg (exactly 0), max |pitch| 17.2 deg, max yaw rate 28 deg/s (limit 3.0 rad/s).
- Clearance: min surface distance 0.34 m; min distance to occupied voxel centre 0.26 m; to voxel box 0.13 m.
  **The 0.3 m voxel clearance requirement is NOT fully met:** 2.2% of samples are below 0.3 m voxel-centre distance, only in passages that are physically ~0.6-1.0 m wide in the scan.

## Rendering
scene_patch meshes and renderer script (copied with one speed-up: Cycles on CPU, 12 samples/pixel instead of 256, because GPU rendering does not fit the 1.5 GB VRAM limit; 12 spp measured ~49 dB PSNR vs 256 spp on test frames), same camera/FOV/resolution/fps, mild unsharp mask as in the patched pipeline, libx264 CRF 18.

## Remaining flaws
Same narrow-doorway limits as cottage_cozy: the second washroom (0.6 m door) and the ensuite are viewed from their doorways. The orbit is an approximate ellipse, not an exact circle.
Visual review was done on contact sheets (frames every 1.5-5 s), not frame-by-frame.
