# restaurant_customer_tour

## How the prompt was interpreted
'Show customers the comfortable, welcoming atmosphere of the eating environment.' Customer-facing spaces only: entrance view, golf-memorabilia booth area, main hall (warm wood tables, pennants, TVs), fish tank, bar with stools, window tables, ending with a long view down the bar room. Kitchen, restrooms and office are deliberately skipped.

## Final trajectory (measured)
- Duration 68.3 s, 1642 frames (24 fps, 960x540, 80 deg FOV), path length 25.3 m.
- Bundle `validate_trajectory`: passed = True (0 occupied voxels, 0 outside grid; gate error ~0).
- Max speed 0.65 m/s (limit 1.0), max accel 0.35 m/s2, max tilt 2.1 deg, thrust/mass 9.8-9.8 m/s2 (daju limits: tilt 30 deg).
- Camera: roll 1.3e-14 deg (exactly 0), max |pitch| 12.1 deg, max yaw rate 25 deg/s (limit 3.0 rad/s).
- Clearance: min surface distance 0.41 m; min distance to occupied voxel centre 0.31 m; to voxel box 0.17 m.
  **The 0.3 m voxel clearance requirement is NOT fully met:** 0.0% of samples are below 0.3 m voxel-centre distance, only in passages that are physically ~0.6-1.0 m wide in the scan.

## Rendering
scene_patch meshes and renderer script (copied with one speed-up: Cycles on CPU, 12 samples/pixel instead of 256, because GPU rendering does not fit the 1.5 GB VRAM limit; 12 spp measured ~49 dB PSNR vs 256 spp on test frames), same camera/FOV/resolution/fps, mild unsharp mask as in the patched pipeline, libx264 CRF 18.

## Remaining flaws
Several window views show the scanned exterior with some artefacts; chair legs are noisy in the scan. Camera height 1.4 m is above seated eye level.
Visual review was done on contact sheets (frames every 1.5-5 s), not frame-by-frame.
