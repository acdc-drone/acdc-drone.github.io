# luxury_show_off

## How the prompt was interpreted
'Drone tour of this house to show off to people.' Highlights: framed-photo entrance gallery hall, the great room with teal sofas and rocking chairs, a reveal from the terrace doors looking back into the room, the kitchen island and cabinets, and the primary bedroom with garden view.

## Final trajectory (measured)
- Duration 123.4 s, 2964 frames (24 fps, 960x540, 80 deg FOV), path length 48.8 m.
- Bundle `validate_trajectory`: passed = True (0 occupied voxels, 0 outside grid; gate error ~0).
- Max speed 0.60 m/s (limit 1.0), max accel 0.34 m/s2, max tilt 2.0 deg, thrust/mass 9.8-9.8 m/s2 (daju limits: tilt 30 deg).
- Camera: roll 2.5e-14 deg (exactly 0), max |pitch| 16.0 deg, max yaw rate 24 deg/s (limit 3.0 rad/s).
- Clearance: min surface distance 0.31 m; min distance to occupied voxel centre 0.20 m; to voxel box 0.06 m.
  **The 0.3 m voxel clearance requirement is NOT fully met:** 2.2% of samples are below 0.3 m voxel-centre distance, only in passages that are physically ~0.6-1.0 m wide in the scan.

## Rendering
scene_patch meshes and renderer script (copied with one speed-up: Cycles on CPU, 12 samples/pixel instead of 256, because GPU rendering does not fit the 1.5 GB VRAM limit; 12 spp measured ~49 dB PSNR vs 256 spp on test frames), same camera/FOV/resolution/fps, mild unsharp mask as in the patched pipeline, libx264 CRF 18.

## Remaining flaws
The terrace/garden beyond the glass is only partly scanned and shows patchy background. The bedroom-wing corridor is narrow and shows wood door panels for a few seconds. The second bedroom and bathrooms are not shown.
Visual review was done on contact sheets (frames every 1.5-5 s), not frame-by-frame.
