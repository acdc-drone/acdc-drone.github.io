## How I read the prompt
The five instructions are followed strictly in order, starting from the entrance:
1. **Dining table:** from just inside the front door, fly forward (west) to the dining area. The camera arcs along the table, which stays framed with the staircase and chandelier.
2. **Living room, circle around:** a full counter-clockwise orbit around the ottoman. The camera faces the room centre throughout, so the sofa wall, patio doors and leather chairs, fireplace/TV, and reading corner each pass through frame.
3. **Bedroom and its washroom:** the drone flies to the mouth of the bedroom passage and holds for 3 s, looking down the passage into the bedroom. **It cannot enter.** The ~0.8 m passage has a best clearance of 0.18 m to the occupied voxels, below the required 0.3 m. The bedroom's washroom cannot be seen from there.
4. **Kitchen and breakfast nook:** the drone takes the only passage with 0.3 m clearance (a 1.1 m corridor beside the bar), sweeps the kitchen, and enters the breakfast nook. There it holds and pans from the round table in the shuttered corner to the workstation.
5. **Second washroom:** the drone returns to the corridor junction and ends looking through the powder-room doorway at its vanity and toilet. The room itself is unreachable (0.05 m clearance).

## Compared with the baseline
The baseline enters the bedroom, its washroom and the powder room, but only by breaking the limits. It reaches up to 1.07 m/s and rolls up to 90°, with pitch from −47° to +48° and yaw rates up to 186°/s. It comes closer than 0.3 m in 32% of frames (min 0.06 m). The near-field critic flags 36% of its frames as mostly blocked within 0.8 m. Ours is within all limits, keeps a level horizon and readable framing, and makes the instruction order obvious. Steps 3 and 5 are shown only from the doorways.

## Remaining flaws
- Steps 3 and 5 are views from doorways, not visits, so the bedroom washroom is not shown at all.
- The kitchen corridor and the powder-room doorway are narrow, so a few seconds show a close door jamb or wall.
