## How I read the prompt
A customer's arrival: start in the entry vestibule, take in the room, glance into the golf-themed side room, then glide through the main dining room at eye level. Next comes the aquarium and the long bar with its TVs and back-bar. At the far end of the bar, the camera looks along the counter and then pans back across the warm, busy-looking room. The pace is unhurried (≤ 0.5 m/s), the horizon is level, and pans are gentle.

Reachability (0.3 m clearance, checked at 5 cm resolution): the vestibule, the main dining room and the bar can be reached. The golf room's only route is a winding 0.7 m-high crawl between booths, so it is shown from its doorway instead. The drone pauses for 4 s and holds the golf room's mural and memorabilia framed in the doorway. The restrooms and kitchen are not reachable (best clearance 0.15–0.25 m) and are not part of this tour.
