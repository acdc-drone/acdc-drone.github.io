## How I read the prompt (and what this house allows)
The prompt describes a floor plan (grand foyer, formal dining room, piano room, staircase, two-storey great room, kitchen and breakfast nook, primary suite with bath and walk-in closet) that does not match this scan. The scanned house is single-storey (ceiling ≈2.5–2.7 m) with no staircase or piano room. With 0.3 m clearance, only the great room, the kitchen and the terraces can be reached. I kept the prompt's **sequence and camera grammar** and mapped each step to the closest real space:
1. **"Grand foyer":** start at the great room's east entrance, next to the passage from the lobby. Hold, **pan left** to the dining area by the west glazing, **pan right** to the lounge/TV wall, then **re-centre**.
2. **"Move forward into the Great Room":** a slow glide to the centre of the great room, with views of the sofa and art wall and the TV wall.
3. **"Turn left into the open Kitchen and Breakfast Nook":** turn and fly into the kitchen, with a hold on the island and table.
4. **"From the Great Room again, turn right into the Primary Bedroom Suite → bath → walk-in closet":** **not reachable**, because the bedroom-wing doorways have 0–0.25 m clearance. The camera returns to the great room and stops at the foyer to look down the bedroom corridor. A terrace-based view of the bedrooms through their glass walls was tried (iter01) and rejected: reflections, a person scanned into the glass, torn geometry.
5. **"Return to the grand foyer":** end on the starting view.

## Compared with the baseline
The baseline (90 s) reaches more rooms, but only by exceeding the limits: up to 1.06 m/s, roll up to 7°, yaw rates up to 266°/s, closer than 0.3 m in 32% of frames. The near-field critic flags 38% of its frames as blocked within 0.8 m. Ours follows the prompt's order and moves (pan left, pan right, re-centre, forward, turn into kitchen, return), stays within every limit, and has no blocked frames.

## Remaining flaws
- The bedroom suite, bath and closet are only indicated by a look down the corridor, not toured.
- There is no real foyer, dining room or piano room; these are mapped to areas of the great room.
- The foyer pans are modest (±25–35°), because the room geometry offers no strongly distinct left and right rooms.
