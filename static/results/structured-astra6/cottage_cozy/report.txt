# cottage_cozy

Video completed and fully decoded for verification.

[Trajectory CSV](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/cottage_cozy/final/cottage_cozy_optimized_path.csv) · [Video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/cottage_cozy/final/cottage_cozy_flythrough.mp4) · [Saved ACDC video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/artifacts/results_xhigh_25k_c8_togt/cottage/cozy/cottage_cozy_flythrough.mp4)

Stage 1: the assistant inspected 64 time-uniform exploration frames, wrote a fresh environment description and 8 shots, and selected matching source frames. Missing pretrained text-encoder weights prevented cosine-similarity retrieval; visual selection among those 64 frames is a substantial deviation. The bundle common.py exported exact recorded poses from the MCAP. No saved ACDC shots or seeds were reused.

Stage 2: all 8 viewpoints received 20 comparisons, rather than the paper’s 100, to meet the morning deadline. The copied GP kernel, Laplace fit and dueling Thompson implementation used 64 candidates, a 1 m translation radius and 30° yaw radius. Yaw-only sampling and zero roll/pitch follow the paper where portable defaults differ. The assistant inspected every pair and recorded the code’s [[1]], [[2]] or [[3]] response. A wrapper fixes the original missing history key and supports restart. Refinement used full textures throughout.

Stage 3: fresh A*, safe-corridor gates and a freshly built TOGT used the original daju parameters and cached occupancy inputs. Yaw is a natural cubic spline of unwrapped headings at optimized gate times. No trajectory was edited or replanned in response to failed checks.

Final rendering uses the patched scene and renderer, full textures, 960×540 at 24 fps, 80° horizontal FOV, H.264 CRF18 and the supplied mild sharpening. CPU rendering used 6 samples per pixel, with the first 576 frames retained at 8, versus the patched default of 256, because measured full-sample throughput could not meet 10:00. Jobs use the shared 3.2 GB queue and two CPU threads.

## Comparison and flaws

34 occupied-voxel samples; speed reaches 1.203 m/s; zero minimum clearance. Three of 12 reviewed frames are dominated by nearby walls/doors; broken stair and furniture geometry remains.

Fresh trajectory duration 82.94 s versus saved 119.33 s. Fresh camera roll/pitch are identically zero; saved CSV maximum absolute roll/pitch are 60.5°/56.0°. I inspected 12 uniformly spaced frames from each video at normalized tour progress (not a full motion review). The fresh samples have level views of the lounge, bedroom, vanity, kitchen and breakfast corner; the saved samples have conspicuous tilted or downward-looking views. Fresh frames 3, 6 and 10 are dominated by nearby walls or doors, so the level horizon does not remove poor transition framing. The fresh ending shows the second washroom; the saved ending looks down toward the breakfast/kitchen floor. Damaged stair rails and furniture remain visible despite the patched scene. Contact sheets and sampled-frame timestamps are retained. Different paths and renderers limit direct image-quality comparison.
