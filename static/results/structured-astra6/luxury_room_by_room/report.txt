# luxury_room_by_room

Video completed and fully decoded for verification.

[Trajectory CSV](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/luxury_room_by_room/final/luxury_room_by_room_optimized_path.csv) · [Video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/luxury_room_by_room/final/luxury_room_by_room_flythrough.mp4) · [Saved ACDC video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/artifacts/results_xhigh_25k_c8_togt/luxury/room_by_room/luxury_room_by_room_flythrough.mp4)

Stage 1: the assistant inspected 64 time-uniform exploration frames, wrote a fresh environment description and 9 shots, and selected matching source frames. Missing pretrained text-encoder weights prevented cosine-similarity retrieval; visual selection among those 64 frames is a substantial deviation. The bundle common.py exported exact recorded poses from the MCAP. No saved ACDC shots or seeds were reused.

Stage 2: all 9 viewpoints received 20 comparisons, rather than the paper’s 100, to meet the morning deadline. The copied GP kernel, Laplace fit and dueling Thompson implementation used 64 candidates, a 1 m translation radius and 30° yaw radius. Yaw-only sampling and zero roll/pitch follow the paper where portable defaults differ. The assistant inspected every pair and recorded the code’s [[1]], [[2]] or [[3]] response. A wrapper fixes the original missing history key and supports restart. Refinement comparisons 1–19 used a 1024-pixel texture cap after memory failures; comparison 20 was rendered again at full texture resolution on both sides. Those earlier decisions were retained.

Stage 3: fresh A*, safe-corridor gates and a freshly built TOGT used the original daju parameters and cached occupancy inputs. Yaw is a natural cubic spline of unwrapped headings at optimized gate times. No trajectory was edited or replanned in response to failed checks.

Final rendering uses the patched scene and renderer, full textures, 960×540 at 24 fps, 80° horizontal FOV, H.264 CRF18 and the supplied mild sharpening. CPU rendering used 6 samples per pixel, versus the patched default of 256, because measured full-sample throughput could not meet 10:00. Jobs use the shared 3.2 GB queue and two CPU threads.

## Comparison and flaws

Speed reaches 1.058 m/s; minimum clearance is 0.013 m. The requested foyer/piano/staircase/closet itinerary is absent; several bedroom/bath transition samples are dominated by walls.

Fresh trajectory duration 85.46 s versus saved 90.44 s. Fresh camera roll/pitch are identically zero; saved CSV maximum absolute roll/pitch are 6.9°/19.3°. I inspected 12 uniformly spaced frames from each video at normalized tour progress (not a full motion review). Both show the open living room, yellow kitchen, hall and bedroom/bath spaces. Fresh samples 6–8 are substantially blocked by nearby walls/doorways; the saved late samples give clearer bathroom and bedroom views. The fresh final view is a hallway, while the saved final view is a bedroom. The grand foyer, formal dining room, piano room, staircase, two-story Great Room and walk-in closet were not identified in the 64 exploration samples. The generated shot list omits those requested spaces, so the literal requested itinerary is not fulfilled; no missing rooms were invented. Both videos still reveal incomplete geometry around the exterior glazing. Contact sheets and timestamps are retained; different paths and renderers limit direct image-quality comparison.
