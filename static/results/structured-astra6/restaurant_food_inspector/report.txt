Video completed and fully decoded for verification.

Stage 1: the assistant inspected 64 time-uniform exploration frames, wrote a fresh environment description and 8 shots, and selected matching source frames. Missing pretrained text-encoder weights prevented cosine-similarity retrieval; visual selection among those 64 frames is a substantial deviation. The bundle common.py exported exact recorded poses from the MCAP. No saved ACDC shots or seeds were reused.

Stage 2: all 8 viewpoints received 20 comparisons, rather than the paper’s 100, to meet the morning deadline. The copied GP kernel, Laplace fit and dueling Thompson implementation used 64 candidates, a 1 m translation radius and 30° yaw radius. Yaw-only sampling and zero roll/pitch follow the paper where portable defaults differ. The assistant inspected every pair and recorded the code’s [[1]], [[2]] or [[3]] response. A wrapper fixes the original missing history key and supports restart. Refinement comparisons 1–19 used a 1024-pixel texture cap after memory failures; comparison 20 was rendered again at full texture resolution on both sides. Those earlier decisions were retained.

Stage 3: fresh A*, safe-corridor gates and a freshly built TOGT used the original daju parameters and cached occupancy inputs. Yaw is a natural cubic spline of unwrapped headings at optimized gate times. No trajectory was edited or replanned in response to failed checks.

Final rendering uses the patched scene and renderer, full textures, 960×540 at 24 fps, 80° horizontal FOV, H.264 CRF18 and the supplied mild sharpening. CPU rendering used 6 samples per pixel, with the first 512 frames retained at 8, versus the patched default of 256, because measured full-sample throughput could not meet 10:00. Jobs use the shared 3.2 GB queue and two CPU threads.

## Comparison and flaws

80 occupied-voxel samples; height reaches −0.283 m; zero minimum clearance. Late samples expose mesh undersides and blank background; clutter and incomplete surfaces undermine the sanitary-presentation goal.

Fresh trajectory duration 62.00 s versus saved 145.49 s. Fresh camera roll/pitch are identically zero; saved CSV maximum absolute roll/pitch are 22.5°/51.0°. I inspected 12 uniformly spaced frames from each video at normalized tour progress (not a full motion review). The fresh tour immediately enters food-service areas and shows drink dispensing, cooking equipment, storage and refrigerators; the saved tour spends its opening samples in dining/restroom circulation before entering the kitchen and ends in the public room. Fresh samples 8–10 show mostly blank background or mesh undersides rather than a usable interior view, consistent with its failed physical checks. The fresh final view shows the washbasin/restroom area. The level camera does not prevent these failures. Clutter and damaged reconstructed surfaces do not convincingly present the requested highly sanitary environment. Contact sheets and timestamps are retained; different paths and renderers limit direct image-quality comparison.
