# restaurant_customer_tour

Video completed and fully decoded for verification.

[Trajectory CSV](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/restaurant_customer_tour/final/restaurant_customer_tour_optimized_path.csv) · [Video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/restaurant_customer_tour/final/restaurant_customer_tour_flythrough.mp4) · [Saved ACDC video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/artifacts/results_xhigh_25k_c8_togt/restaurant/customer_tour/restaurant_customer_tour_flythrough.mp4)

Stage 1: the assistant inspected 64 time-uniform exploration frames, wrote a fresh environment description and 7 shots, and selected matching source frames. Missing pretrained text-encoder weights prevented cosine-similarity retrieval; visual selection among those 64 frames is a substantial deviation. The bundle common.py exported exact recorded poses from the MCAP. No saved ACDC shots or seeds were reused.

Stage 2: all 7 viewpoints received 20 comparisons, rather than the paper’s 100, to meet the morning deadline. The copied GP kernel, Laplace fit and dueling Thompson implementation used 64 candidates, a 1 m translation radius and 30° yaw radius. Yaw-only sampling and zero roll/pitch follow the paper where portable defaults differ. The assistant inspected every pair and recorded the code’s [[1]], [[2]] or [[3]] response. A wrapper fixes the original missing history key and supports restart. Refinement comparisons 1–19 used a 1024-pixel texture cap after memory failures; comparison 20 was rendered again at full texture resolution on both sides. Those earlier decisions were retained.

Stage 3: fresh A*, safe-corridor gates and a freshly built TOGT used the original daju parameters and cached occupancy inputs. Yaw is a natural cubic spline of unwrapped headings at optimized gate times. No trajectory was edited or replanned in response to failed checks.

Final rendering uses the patched scene and renderer, full textures, 960×540 at 24 fps, 80° horizontal FOV, H.264 CRF18 and the supplied mild sharpening. CPU rendering used 6 samples per pixel, versus the patched default of 256, because measured full-sample throughput could not meet 10:00. Jobs use the shared 3.2 GB queue and two CPU threads. The customer-tour scene was losslessly unpacked into glTF with original image bytes to avoid packed-image duplication after a capped-memory failure.

## Comparison and flaws

Speed reaches 1.098 m/s; minimum voxel clearance is 0.077 m. Sampled views show broken chair/table geometry and some tight wall/bar framing.

Fresh trajectory duration 45.63 s versus saved 51.39 s. Fresh camera roll/pitch are identically zero; saved CSV maximum absolute roll/pitch are 7.3°/18.0°. I inspected 12 uniformly spaced frames from each video at normalized tour progress (not a full motion review). Both show the entrance, sports-photo dining area, main tables and bar. The fresh tour keeps a level horizon, has broader bar-shelf views near the end and ends facing along the public room; the saved tour ends on a tighter television/beer-sign corner. Fresh samples still show incomplete chair and table meshes and prominent foreground walls at the entrance. Different trajectory durations and the patched rendering mean this is not a controlled image-quality comparison. Contact sheets and sampled-frame timestamps are retained in comparison_frames/ and comparison.json.
