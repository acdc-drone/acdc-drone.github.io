# cottage_step_by_step

Video completed and fully decoded for verification.

[Trajectory CSV](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/cottage_step_by_step/final/cottage_step_by_step_optimized_path.csv) · [Video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/horizontal/structured-astra6/runs/cottage_step_by_step/final/cottage_step_by_step_flythrough.mp4) · [Saved ACDC video](/home/ran/Downloads/ddc_portable/artifacts/results_xhigh_25k_c8_togt/cottage/step_by_step/cottage_step_by_step_flythrough.mp4)

Stage 1: the assistant inspected 64 time-uniform exploration frames, wrote a fresh environment description and 10 shots, and selected matching source frames. Missing pretrained text-encoder weights prevented cosine-similarity retrieval; visual selection among those 64 frames is a substantial deviation. The bundle common.py exported exact recorded poses from the MCAP. No saved ACDC shots or seeds were reused.

Stage 2: all 10 viewpoints received 20 comparisons, rather than the paper’s 100, to meet the morning deadline. The copied GP kernel, Laplace fit and dueling Thompson implementation used 64 candidates, a 1 m translation radius and 30° yaw radius. Yaw-only sampling and zero roll/pitch follow the paper where portable defaults differ. The assistant inspected every pair and recorded the code’s [[1]], [[2]] or [[3]] response. A wrapper fixes the original missing history key and supports restart. Refinement used full textures throughout.

Stage 3: fresh A*, safe-corridor gates and a freshly built TOGT used the original daju parameters and cached occupancy inputs. Yaw is a natural cubic spline of unwrapped headings at optimized gate times. No trajectory was edited or replanned in response to failed checks.

Final rendering uses the patched scene and renderer, full textures, 960×540 at 24 fps, 80° horizontal FOV, H.264 CRF18 and the supplied mild sharpening. CPU rendering used 6 samples per pixel, versus the patched default of 256, because measured full-sample throughput could not meet 10:00. Jobs use the shared 3.2 GB queue and two CPU threads.

## Comparison and flaws

23 occupied-voxel samples; speed reaches 1.053 m/s; zero minimum clearance. Starts close to the dining table, has tight television/bed framing and wall/door-dominated transition samples.

Fresh trajectory duration 80.08 s versus saved 133.92 s. Fresh camera roll/pitch are identically zero; saved CSV maximum absolute roll/pitch are 90.0°/47.5°. I inspected 12 uniformly spaced frames from each video at normalized tour progress (not a full motion review). The fresh samples follow dining, living, bedroom/bath, kitchen/breakfast and second-washroom order, with a level horizon. They start close to the dining table rather than clearly establishing an entrance approach; the sampled views do not establish a full living-room circle. A close television view, partially cropped bed and wall/door-dominated frames 6 and 10 weaken the tour. The saved samples show stronger tilt and several floor/ceiling-directed views; its final sample looks from the washroom into the corridor, while the fresh final sample centers the vanity. Reconstruction defects remain. Contact sheets and timestamps are retained; different paths and renderers limit direct image-quality comparison.
